# SpaceShrink Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 September 2026

**Developer:** Tejinder Pal Singh

This privacy policy explains how SpaceShrink handles information when you use the Android application.

## 1. Overview

SpaceShrink is designed as an on-device storage and file utility. Core media compression and conversion features are intended to operate locally on your Android device.

The current application manifest does not request the `INTERNET` permission. The current release does not include a developer-operated analytics server, advertising SDK, or developer-operated cloud storage service.

## 2. Files and media

SpaceShrink may request access to photos and videos so that you can select, analyze, compress, convert, review, or delete files you choose.

The app uses Android media APIs and user/system consent mechanisms where applicable. The app does not need unrestricted access to every file on the device in order to use its core media features.

Files selected for processing may be copied to temporary application storage while a compression or conversion operation is running. Temporary files are intended to be removed after processing when they are no longer needed.

## 3. Compressed and converted files

When you ask SpaceShrink to create a compressed or converted file, the app may save the result to a location such as the device's media library or its SpaceShrink output folder.

The original file is not intended to be silently overwritten by the core compression workflow. Destructive actions are presented for user review and, where supported, use Android's system trash/delete consent mechanisms.

## 4. Vault

The Private Vault stores protected content in app-controlled storage and uses Android biometric authentication where available. Vault content is intended to remain on the device unless you explicitly export or share it.

## 5. Storage and preferences

SpaceShrink stores application preferences, compression history and other local app state required to operate the features you enable. These records are stored on the device.

## 6. Sharing and external apps

When you use Android's Share function, the selected file is handed to the external app you choose. That external app may process the file under its own privacy policy and terms.

## 7. Google Play Billing

The project contains an optional Google Play Billing integration for releases distributed through Google Play. If a future release activates billing, purchase processing is handled through Google Play and is subject to Google's terms and privacy practices. Direct APK releases outside Google Play may not have the same billing functionality.

## 8. Children's privacy

SpaceShrink is not specifically directed to children. Do not provide personal information to the developer through support channels unless it is necessary to resolve a support request.

## 9. Data security

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You should keep your Android device protected with a screen lock and maintain backups of important data.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SpaceShrink's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions, security issues, or support, use the contact method published on the official SpaceShrink website or GitHub repository.

This document is a practical project privacy notice and is not legal advice. Before distributing the app commercially or adding analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud storage, or payment services, review and update this policy for the exact services and jurisdictions involved.
